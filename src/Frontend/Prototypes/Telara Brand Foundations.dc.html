Color — verified against WCAG AAA

Your blue/green concept, checked pixel-by-pixel: I computed contrast ratios for every tone against both mode backgrounds and picked the lightness step that clears 7:1 (AAA for body text) on each. Same hue, different lightness per mode — light tones read as text on dark, dark tones read as text on light.
